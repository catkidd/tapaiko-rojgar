01 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile phone) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, remember your preferences, and provide reporting information to website owners.

Similar technologies include:

  • Local Storage: Browser-based storage we use for saving job bookmarks and search preferences without expiry
  • Session Storage: Temporary storage cleared when you close your browser tab
  • Pixel Tags / Web Beacons: Tiny transparent images used to track email open rates (in our job alert emails)

02 Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies Always Active

These cookies are strictly necessary for the website to function. Without them, you cannot log in, maintain a session, or use the search filters. They cannot be disabled.

Analytics Cookies Optional

Help us understand how visitors interact with the platform — which features are most used, where users drop off, and how we can improve. We use Google Analytics with IP anonymization.

Preference Cookies Optional

Remember your language preference, search filter settings, and whether you have dismissed certain notifications. These make the Platform more convenient to use.

Marketing Cookies Optional

Used to deliver more relevant job recommendations and, on partner sites, tailored advertisements. These are only placed with your explicit consent.

Additionally, we use localStorage (not a cookie) to save your bookmarked jobs. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

04 Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies on our Platform are set by third-party services we use:

  • Google Analytics — Website analytics. Privacy-safe with IP anonymization enabled. Google Privacy Policy ↗
  • Google Maps — Embedded on our Contact page to show our office location. Google Privacy Policy ↗
  • Khalti / eSewa — Payment processors for employer subscriptions. Their own cookie/privacy policies apply. Khalti Privacy Policy ↗
  • Facebook Pixel — Marketing analytics (consent required). Facebook Privacy Policy ↗

We do not control third-party cookies and encourage you to review their privacy policies separately.

05 Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You can update your cookie preferences at any time using the Cookie Preference Center at the top of this page. Your choices are saved in the tr_cookie_consent cookie for 1 year.

Available options:

  • Accept All: Enable all cookie categories for the best experience
  • Save My Preferences: Enable only the categories you have toggled on
  • Reject Non-Essential: Use only strictly necessary cookies

06 Browser Settings

You can also control cookies through your browser settings. Here's how in popular browsers:

Google Chrome Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies
Mozilla Firefox Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies
Safari Preferences → Privacy → Cookies
Microsoft Edge Settings → Cookies and site permissions
Blocking all cookies may affect your ability to log in, use search filters, and save jobs. We recommend allowing at least Essential cookies for full functionality.

07 Do Not Track

Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" (DNT) signal. As there is no universally accepted standard for how websites should respond to DNT signals, we do not currently alter our data collection practices in response to DNT signals. However, you can manage your preferences through our Cookie Preference Center above.

08 Policy Updates

We may update this Cookie Policy to reflect changes in the cookies we use or applicable law. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date. We will display a notification on the Platform when changes are significant.
