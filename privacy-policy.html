01 Who We Are

Tapaiko Rojgar Pvt. Ltd. ("Tapaiko Rojgar", "we", "us", or "our") is a registered technology company based in Kathmandu, Nepal. We operate the job portal and recruitment platform at tapaikorojgar.com.np.

As the data controller, we are responsible for determining how and why your personal data is processed. Our registered address is:

02 Data We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly and data collected automatically during your use of our platform:

Information You Provide

  • Account Data: Full name, email address, phone number, password (hashed)
  • Profile Data: CV/resume, work experience, education history, skills, profile photo
  • Employment Preferences: Job type, location preference, salary expectations, availability
  • Employer Data: Company name, registration number, PAN/VAT number, company logo, job descriptions
  • Communication Data: Messages sent via our platform, support tickets, contact form submissions
  • Payment Data: Billing name, address (payment card details are processed by our payment processor and not stored by us)

Data Collected Automatically

  • Usage Data: Pages visited, features used, search queries, job applications submitted
  • Device Data: IP address, browser type, operating system, screen resolution
  • Cookies: Session cookies, preference cookies, analytics cookies (see Section 5)
  • Log Data: Access timestamps, error logs, referral URLs

03 How We Use Your Data

We process your data on the following legal bases and for these purposes:

Purpose Legal Basis Examples
Service Delivery Contract performance Matching candidates to jobs, processing applications
Account Management Contract performance Login, profile management, notifications
Platform Improvement Legitimate interests Analytics, A/B testing, bug fixing
Marketing Communications Consent Job alerts, newsletters, promotional emails

04 Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only in these circumstances:

  • Employers: When you apply for a job, your profile and CV are shared with the relevant employer. You control which information is visible.
  • Service Providers: We use trusted third-party providers for cloud hosting (AWS Nepal), analytics (Google Analytics), email delivery (SendGrid), and payment processing (Khalti/eSewa). These providers are contractually bound to protect your data.
  • Legal Authorities: We may disclose data to government authorities when required by law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of our users.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger or acquisition, your data may be transferred to the successor entity under equivalent privacy protections.

05 Cookies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies. For full details, please read our Cookie Policy. In summary:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for basic site functionality (login sessions, security tokens). Cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how users interact with our platform (Google Analytics). You can opt out.
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings and job search preferences.
  • Marketing Cookies: Used for relevant job alert targeting. Requires your explicit consent.

You can manage cookie preferences via your browser settings or our Cookie Consent Manager.

06 Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal data:

  • All data transmission encrypted via TLS 1.3 / HTTPS
  • Passwords hashed using bcrypt with salting
  • Database encryption at rest (AES-256)
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing
  • Strict employee access controls with multi-factor authentication
  • Automated threat detection and incident response procedures
While we take all reasonable steps to protect your data, no internet transmission is 100% secure. Please use a strong, unique password for your Tapaiko Rojgar account and never share it with others.

07 Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary for the purposes stated in this policy:

  • Active Accounts: Retained for the duration of your account plus 2 years after last activity
  • Job Applications: Retained for 3 years from application date
  • Financial Records: Retained for 7 years as required by Nepalese tax law
  • Log Data: Retained for 12 months for security and debugging purposes
  • Deleted Accounts: Account data removed within 30 days of deletion request; anonymized analytics data may be retained longer

08 Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

Right to Access

Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you.

Right to Rectification

Correct inaccurate or incomplete personal data.

Right to Erasure

Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten").

Right to Object

Object to processing of your data for marketing or profiling.

Right to Portability

Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.

Right to Restriction

Restrict how we process your data in certain circumstances.

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@tapaikorojgar.com.np. We will respond within 30 days.

09 Children's Privacy

Tapaiko Rojgar is not intended for individuals under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a minor, please contact us immediately at privacy@tapaikorojgar.com.np and we will promptly delete it.

10 Third-Party Links

Our platform may contain links to external websites and employer company pages. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third parties. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing any personal information to them.

11 Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. When we make material changes, we will:

  • Update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page
  • Send a notification email to all registered users
  • Display a banner on the platform for 30 days following significant changes

Continued use of our platform after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
