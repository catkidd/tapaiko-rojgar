## 1. Brand Essence & Core Vision

**\"Tapaiko Rojgar\"** (translated from Nepali as *\"Your Employment\"*)
is a high-authority digital platform dedicated to recruitment,
professional networking, and institutional talent acquisition. The
visual language bridges global standards with local execution.

::: highlight-box
**The Core Metaphor:** The identity fuses a corporate recruiter\'s
data-driven precision (the checklist/charts) with human ambition and
modern global reach (the human ascending toward a star against a world
map background). Every web layout must echo this professional, highly
trustworthy, yet aspirational theme.
:::

## 2. Visual Logo Architecture & Breakdown

To accurately guide AI generation models or designers, the visual logo
asset from the primary brand mark is divided into 4 specific
sub-components:

1.  **The Human Ascendance & Star:** A stylized blue silhouette of a
    professional leaping upwards, hands transforming into an arrow
    reaching a gold 5-pointed star. This represents career mobility,
    success, and high-tier placement.
2.  **The Global Network:** A desaturated, abstract latitude/longitude
    world map backdrop bounded within a circular arc, implying global
    placement opportunities and world-class technology.
3.  **The Data & Verification Suite:**
    -   A clean CV/Resume clipboard on the left containing profile
        pictures and structured form fields, signifying verified data,
        screening, and legal readiness.
    -   An ascending 3-bar growth chart on the right side, signaling
        continuous career growth and economic progress.
4.  **The Typography Wordmark:** The brand name **\"Tapaiko\"** in a
    heavy, geometric sans-serif font (Deep Navy) with the word **\"---
    Rojgar ---\"** balanced perfectly underneath in a clean, tracked-out
    gold sans-serif uppercase text enclosed by geometric rules.

## 4. Typography Hierarchy for Web

To retain the geometric and clean aesthetic of the corporate identity,
the digital interface uses standard web-safe and Google-accessible
typefaces:

-   **Primary Headers (H1, H2, H3):** Montserrat or Poppins (Weights:
    600, 700 Bold). Geometric, clean, authoritative.
-   **Body Text, Buttons, Form Fields:** Inter or Helvetica Neue
    (Weights: 400 Regular, 500 Medium). Optimized for reading heavy
    lists of jobs and user resumes.

::: spec-box
/\* AI CSS Ingestion Baseline \*/ \--primary-blue: #002244;
\--accent-gold: #b8860b; \--accent-gold-light: #d4af37; \--text-dark:
#0f172a; \--bg-light: #f8fafc; h1, h2, h3 { font-family: \'Montserrat\',
sans-serif; color: var(\--primary-blue); } body { font-family:
\'Inter\', sans-serif; color: var(\--text-dark); }
:::

## 5. Digital Platform UI Layout Strategy

When feeding these guidelines to a layout generation AI or building the
UI, apply these strict architectural layouts based on the brand\'s logo
characteristics:

::: layout-table
::: layout-row
::: layout-column
### Hero Section Layout

The Hero section must feature a split structure. Left side: Bold H1
header in Deep Navy outlining employment success, backed by an Executive
Gold CTA button. Right side: A modern card-stack illustration embodying
clean candidate profiles and growth graphs (directly mimicking the
clipboard and bar chart design elements).
:::

::: layout-column
### Card & Component Architecture

All functional items (such as job listings, resumes, and corporate
details) must be wrapped inside clean, pure-white cards with micro-drop
shadows. Use `border-radius: 8px` to mirror the rounded borders of the
brand\'s corporate icon spheres.
:::
:::
:::

## 6. Direct Prompt Formula for Website AI Engines

Copy and paste the exact text snippet below directly into any
UI-generation AI tool (such as v0.dev, Claude Engineer, ChatGPT, or
Tailwind Builders) to achieve immediate compliance without conceptual
drift:

::: {.spec-box style="background-color: #002244; color: #ffffff; border-left: 4px solid #d4af37; font-family: sans-serif; font-size: 10pt;"}
\"Create a highly professional Job Portal and Recruitment Website under
the corporate brand \'Tapaiko Rojgar\'. CRITICAL THEME & COLOR
COMPLIANCE: - Use an elite corporate recruitment aesthetic. - Primary
Color: Deep Navy Blue (#002244) used for navigation bars, main hero
headings, and footers. - Accent Color: Executive Metallic Gold gradient
(#D4AF37 to #B8860B) used strictly for high-priority Call-To-Actions
(CTAs), feature highlights, and active states. - Backgrounds: Clean
crisp white backgrounds with soft slate-grey borders for job cards. KEY
UI UTILITIES & SECTIONS REQUIRED: 1. Navigation: Sticky bar featuring
the dark corporate logo on the left, primary navigation links in the
center, and a \'Post a Job\' accent-gold button on the right. 2. Hero
Section: Left column features bold geometric headings with integrated
job search filters. Right column features a clean digital mockup
showcasing candidate checklists and career tracking graphs. 3. Feature
Layout: A multi-column responsive grid showcasing job cards. Each card
must feature high padding, minimal thin borders, a modern typography
layout (using Montserrat for titles and Inter for text), and soft
rounded borders. DO NOT use vibrant neon colors, generic light-blue tech
accents, or messy layouts. The page must project elite authority, job
market safety, data verification, and career progression.\"
:::
